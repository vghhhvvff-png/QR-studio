GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Are my QR codes static or dynamic?

QR Studio creates static QR codes. Their content is embedded directly in the pattern and cannot be changed after creation. To update the content, create and share a new QR code.

Is my QR code encrypted?

No. QR codes are not encrypted. Anyone with a camera or scanner can read the content encoded in one. Avoid putting passwords, private keys, or sensitive personal data in a QR code you share publicly.

Can a logo or color make my QR code harder to scan?

Yes. Low contrast, decorative styles, and logos covering too many modules can reduce scan reliability. Keep a clear border, use strong contrast, and test the exported code on multiple phones before printing.

Can I use QR Studio offline?

After the required site files have loaded, QR generation and image processing work in your browser without a network connection. A first visit still needs to load the site files; QR Studio does not promise offline access before that.
